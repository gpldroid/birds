The barn owl is a nocturnal hunter found across many parts of the world. Its heart-shaped facial disc helps channel sound toward its ears, supporting precise listening in darkness.

Barn owls often hunt over open ground, where small mammals can form a large part of their diet. Old buildings, tree cavities and purpose-built nest boxes may provide nesting opportunities where suitable habitat remains.
