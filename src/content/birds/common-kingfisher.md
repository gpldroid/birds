The common kingfisher is known for its vivid blue and orange plumage. It often waits on a branch or other perch above water before diving to catch small fish and aquatic invertebrates.

Clear water and suitable bankside vegetation are important to its way of life. When watching kingfishers, keep well back from likely nesting banks and avoid disturbing feeding sites.
