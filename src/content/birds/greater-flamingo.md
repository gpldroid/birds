The greater flamingo is a highly social bird that often gathers in large flocks in shallow wetlands. Its specialised bill filters tiny food items from water and mud.

Flamingos depend on suitable wetland habitats for feeding and breeding. Water quality and stable wetland conditions are important for their colonies.
