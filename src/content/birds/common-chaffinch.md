The common chaffinch is a widespread finch found across much of Europe and parts of western Asia and North Africa. It uses a variety of wooded habitats, including parks and gardens with mature trees.

Chaffinches feed mainly on seeds and other plant material, while small invertebrates are important at certain times of year, especially when feeding young.
