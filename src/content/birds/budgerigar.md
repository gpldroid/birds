The budgerigar is a small parakeet native to Australia. In the wild, it is adapted to dry open country and may travel in flocks in search of grass seeds and water.

Its popularity as a companion bird makes responsible care especially important. Pet birds need suitable space, social interaction, enrichment, a balanced diet and access to an avian veterinarian.
