The peregrine falcon is one of the most widespread birds of prey. It occurs in a remarkable range of landscapes, from rugged sea cliffs to city centres where tall buildings can provide nesting ledges.

Peregrines usually hunt other birds in flight. Their streamlined shape, pointed wings and powerful flight make them exceptionally capable aerial hunters.
